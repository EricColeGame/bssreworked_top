## Overview of Field Mechanics in BSS Reworked

Fields in *BSS Reworked* are designated grid zones filled with flowers of varying colors, sizes, and tiers. When you swing your collection tools or direct your bee swarm into a field, flower petals are harvested to fill your container with raw pollen, which is later converted into Honey at your hive.

Each field operates on specific environmental rules:

- **Flower Colors:** Red, Blue, and White flowers dictate which bee types perform best. Red bees excel in red flower fields, blue bees dominate blue patches, and colorless bees offer versatile general gathering.
- **Flower Tiers:** Flowers range from small single petals to double and triple-tier blossoms. Higher-tier flowers yield significantly higher base pollen per gather tick.
- **Field Boosts & Winds:** Active modifiers grant massive multipliers (e.g., 4x Field Boost, 15x Winds, and Market Boosts), dramatically scaling your collection rate.
- **Zone Locks:** Higher elevation sections of the mountain require minimum bee counts in your hive before the entry gates open.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the best BSS Reworked fields for beginners?
The best starting fields are Sunflower Field, Mushroom Field, and Clover Field. These areas have no aggressive high-level mobs, feature rapid flower regeneration, and allow new players to complete starting bear quests quickly.

### How do flower colors affect my farming route?
Flower colors determine which bees collect pollen most efficiently. If your hive consists primarily of red bees, you will gather substantially more pollen in red fields like Strawberry Field and Pepper Patch compared to blue zones like Pine Tree Forest.

### What is the fastest way to unlock higher BSS Reworked fields?
The fastest method is buying Basic and Silver Eggs, hatching new bees to increase your total hive count, and crossing the gate thresholds (5, 10, 15, 25, and 35 bees). Completing daily bear quests provides the Honey necessary for expanding hive slots.

### Can field boosts from codes stack with booster dispensers?
Yes. Field boosts granted via promotional codes, field booster dispensers, wind shrines, and Honeyday event multipliers stack together, allowing your pollen per second to reach peak efficiency.
