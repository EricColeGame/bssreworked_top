## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best BSS Reworked areas for early honey farming?
For absolute beginners, the Clover Field and Spider Field (unlocked at 5 bees) provide the highest hybrid pollen density and critical strike multipliers. Once you hit 15 bees, transition your farming runs to the Pine Tree Forest for blue pollen or Rose Field for red pollen.

### How do field mechanics differ in BSS Reworked compared to standard versions?
In this reworked experience, flower regrowth rates, mob respawn timers, and token link speeds have been fine-tuned for smoother progression. Certain fields also feature revised color ratios to balance mixed-hive viability across all tiers.

### Can I transfer data into BSS Reworked?
According to the game's official description and community notices, players can recover specific data from compatible 1:1 legacy versions created by community developers, allowing returning players to preserve previous grinding milestones.

### When should I unlock the 35-Bee gate zones?
You should aim to unlock the 35-bee gate as soon as your hive average reaches level 8 to 9. While the Coconut Crab boss requires higher levels to defeat, unlocking the area early gives you immediate access to Spirit Bear quests and the Wind Shrine favor mechanics.
