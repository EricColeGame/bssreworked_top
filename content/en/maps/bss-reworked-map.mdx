## Overview of Major Layout Shifts and Additions

The progression structure of the mountain revolves around gate thresholds dictated by the size of your bee swarm. Over time, structural adjustments have expanded several classic areas while adding dedicated zones to handle multiplayer systems without cluttering standard fields.

```
       [ 35-Bee Zone: Coconut Field, Pepper Patch, Spirit Bear ]
                                |
       [ 30-Bee Zone: Mountain Top Field, Wind Shrine, Meteors ]
                                |
       [ 20-Bee & 25-Bee Zones: Pine Tree, Pumpkin, Rose, Ant Gate ]
                                |
       [ 10-Bee & 15-Bee Zones: Cactus, Spider, Bamboo, Strawberry ]
                                |
       [ Starter Zone (0-5 Bees): Hive Hub Portal, Starter Fields ]
```

One of the most defining additions to the game's overall footprint is the separate server architecture known as the **Hive Hub**. Accessible through a portal situated past the Ant Gate area, the Hive Hub allows players to interact in a specialized space dedicated to trading stickers and Beequips without burdening the mountain server performance.

### Key Structural Changes Over Time

* **Relocated Challenge Portals**: Portals like the Retro Swarm Challenge have found permanent positions near utility areas such as Dapper Bear's Shop.
* **Repositioned Utility Machines**: The Mythic Meteor Shower summoner was moved to the roof of the Badge Bearer's Guild to improve accessibility and line of sight.
* **Streamlined Secret Pathways**: The treacherous lava obstacle course (obby) located behind the Honey Dispenser received structural widening and clearer landing zones to make navigation less frustrating for mobile and PC players alike.
* **Collision & Geometry Polish**: Unnecessary hitbox snags—such as tight gaps between trees in the Pine Tree Forest and solid collision on the massive pumpkin in the Pumpkin Patch—were reworked to promote fluid movement collection.

## Starter and Mid-Tier Zones: Fields, Hazards, and Access Points

The lower tiers of the mountain are built for foundational gathering and early quest completion. While straightforward, subtle adjustments have refined how players loop through these early zones.

```
  [ Blue Flower Field ] ----- [ Clover Field ] ----- [ Mushroom Field ]
             \                       |                      /
              \--- [ Starter Shop / Hive Platforms ] ------/
                                     |
                         [ Sunflower / Dandelion ]
```

### 0 to 5 Bee Starter Area
The lowest altitude contains five primary fields featuring low-tier mob spawns (Ladybugs and Beetles). 
* **Sunflower Field**: Dominated by yellow flowers, perfect for early colorless pollen tasks and Sunflower Badges.
* **Dandelion Field**: Offers light white flower farming and quick access to the main hive platforms.
* **Mushroom Field**: Heavy red concentration; ideal for early Red Boost stacking.
* **Blue Flower Field**: The fundamental starting ground for Blue Boost gathering.
* **Clover Field**: A balanced multi-colored field where early Sprout pops and Beetle battles frequently occur.

### 10 to 15 Bee Mid-Tier Area
Passing the 10-bee threshold brings higher enemy threats and specialized pollen patches:
* **Spider Field**: White flower territory guarded by the Spider mini-boss.
* **Bamboo Field**: Blue flower dense area often frequented by low-level Aphids.
* **Strawberry Field**: Red-dominated field sitting right next to the path leading up to the 15-bee ledge.
* **Utility Placements**: The Blender and Wealth Clock are critical checkpoints here, allowing players to run crafting queues and claim ticket rewards between field rotations.

## Mobility Upgrades, Shortcuts, and Farming Loops

To maximize pollen-per-second conversion, you must construct a movement loop that minimizes dead travel time between fields, dispensers, and your hive claim.

```
  [ Summit: Coconut/Pepper ] 
             |  (Parachute / Glider Descent)
             v
  [ Plateau: Mountain Top Booster ] 
             |  (Glide Downwards)
             v
  [ Mid Tier: Pine / Pumpkin / Blender ] 
             |  (Cannon / Jump Pad Return)
             v
  [ Base Hive Platforms ]
```

### Top Farming Loop Strategies

#### 1. The Red Booster Rotation
* **Route**: Red HQ $\rightarrow$ Rose Field $\rightarrow$ Mushroom Field $\rightarrow$ Pepper Patch (via Red Cannon).
* **Efficiency Tip**: Trigger the Red Field Booster at Red HQ first. If it hits Pepper Patch or Rose, use your glider from the top cannons to immediately land inside the boosted flower patches without running up ramps.

#### 2. The Blue Pine Forest Loop
* **Route**: Blue HQ $\rightarrow$ Bamboo Field $\rightarrow$ Pine Tree Forest $\rightarrow$ Stump Field (if Snail is cleared).
* **Efficiency Tip**: Take advantage of the widened clearing between the pine trees. Deploy Bubble Balloons or Frogs at the edge of the field where flower density is highest to maximize your conversion radius.

#### 3. The Utility Dispenser Sweep
* **Route**: Wealth Clock $\rightarrow$ Ticket Tent $\rightarrow$ Memory Match (Night/Mountain) $\rightarrow$ Blender.
* **Schedule**: Run this route every 45 to 60 minutes. Combining your cooldown timers ensures you never lose stacked Wealth Clock buffs while keeping your crafting queue constantly fed with extracts and glues.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the biggest changes in the BSS reworked map?
The biggest changes in the **BSS reworked map** include the relocation of the Retro Swarm Challenge portal next to Dapper Bear's Shop, moving the Mythic Meteor Shower summoning platform to the roof of the Badge Bearer's Guild, smoothing out the geometry in Pine Tree Forest, and adding the dedicated Hive Hub teleporter past the Ant Gate.

### How do I access the Hive Hub in the updated map layout?
You can access the Hive Hub by walking past the 20-Bee zone entrance toward the Ant Challenge Gate. Look for the glowing portal frame along the cliffside wall. Stepping into this portal transfers your character into the multiplayer trade lounge without despawning your mountain data.

### Where is the easiest path to reach the Mountain Top Field?
The fastest route to the Mountain Top Field is by launching yourself from the Yellow Cannon located in the 15-Bee zone ledge, or by utilizing the Red/Blue slingshots combined with a Glider or Parachute to drift onto the 30-Bee plateau.

### Did field flower layouts change with the map reworks?
Yes. Several fields received adjustments to their mono-color flower ratios and spatial boundaries. For example, Stump Field was shifted to have a stronger blue flower concentration, and collision boxes on center props (like the giant pumpkin) were removed to ensure auto-gather tools and ability tokens do not get blocked.
