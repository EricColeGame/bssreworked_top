## Starter & Early Game Locations (0 to 10 Bee Zones)

The early game focuses on establishing your foundational swarm and gathering early tools. Exploring the lower **BSS Reworked locations** quickly reveals free Royal Jelly drops and starter tickets tucked behind geometry.

### The Hive Valley (0 Bee Area)
The central valley contains your hive slots, the Noob Shop, and the foundational flower patches. 

*   **Sunflower Field:** High concentration of white pollen; ideal for early basic quests.
*   **Dandelion Field:** Balanced mix of white and blue pollen.
*   **Mushroom Field:** The primary early-game red pollen source; watch out for Ladybugs.
*   **Clover Field:** A multi-colored clover patch surrounded by hidden platforming ramps.

The most reliable secret Diamond Egg is located in the lower void ledges underneath the high-tier zones. You will need a Glider and high movement speed to safely glide down to the hidden platform without falling into the map reset boundary.

### What is the most efficient travel path between major BSS Reworked locations?
Using the high ground of the Mountain Top (25-Bee zone) as a launchpad is the fastest travel method. By gliding down from the peak, you can reach almost any mid or low-tier field in seconds without running across gates.

### Do data transfers affect which map locations I can access?
