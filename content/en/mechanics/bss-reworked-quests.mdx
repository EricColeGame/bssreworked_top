## Core Progression Bears: Main Storyline Quests

The foundational progression in *Bee Swarm Simulator* revolves around fixed questlines offered by key bear NPCs located across various zones. Completing these finite storylines provides essential stepping stones, moving players from basic equipment to elite endgame gear.

### Black Bear: The Journey to Mythic Eggs

Black Bear serves as the central pollen milestone tracker. Historically, his quest chain ended early, but recent structural updates introduced high-tier questlines that culminate in massive rewards, including Star Jellies and a guaranteed Mythic Egg.

## Repeatable and Scaling Quests: The Infinite Grind

Once standard questlines wrap up, repeatable quests provide ongoing resource generation. These systems feature distinct mechanics, cooldowns, and dynamic math.

### Black Bear's New Repeatable System

After finishing the Mythic Egg storyline, Black Bear switches into a specialized repeatable quest giver. Unlike old repeatable iterations, which gave small sums of pollen tasks for minor honey payouts, the new format aligns with endgame economy requirements.

* **Cooldown:** 1 quest per hour.
* **Requirements:** 50M to 500M pollen across specific fields or color categories.
* **Guaranteed Payouts:** 50M to 200M Honey, Tickets, Royal Jellies, and assorted crafting fruits.

```
Old Repeatable Average: 1,000,000 Pollen  -> 500,000 Honey
New Repeatable Average: 250,000,000 Pollen -> 100,000,000 Honey + High-Tier Drops
```

### Brown Bear Scaling Formulas and Fixed Milestones

Brown Bear operates on a mathematical scaling algorithm. For players under 1,000 completed quests, required pollen scales exponentially via a power curve:

$$\text{Required Pollen} = x + (y - x) \times \left(\frac{\text{Quests Completed}}{1000}\right)^4$$

*(Where $x$ is the minimum base pollen and $y$ is the maximum theoretical pollen cap).*

### Polar Bear and Dapper Bear Utility Tasks

* **Polar Bear:** Focuses on hunting recipes (e.g., *Spooky Stew*, *Extreme Stir-Fry*). Each turn-in grants **Polar Power**, permanently increasing your hive's Bee Energy capacity.

### Specialized Color Bee Quests (Bucko, Riley, Honey Bee)

Once Translators are delivered to the Blue and Red HQs, Gifted Bucko Bee and Gifted Riley Bee offer color-focused tasks that scale dynamically with your completion tally.

* **Gifted Bucko Bee:** Blue Boosts, Pine Tree/Bamboo gathering, Blueberry feeding, and Rhino Beetle hunts.

* **Honey Bee:** Assigns *Honey Bee's Honey Hunt*, scaling by $500 \times n$ Honey Tokens. Every 10th completion yields a guaranteed Star Jelly and a temporary 2x Convert Rate boost.

## Tips and Strategies for Completing Quests Faster

Optimizing your daily routine allows you to finish multiple objectives simultaneously without wasting materials or time.

```
       [Accept All Available Bear & Bee Quests]
                         |
       [Identify Overlapping Field Requirements]
                         |
    +--------------------+--------------------+
    |                                         |
[Primary Field Farming]              [Cooldown Rotation]
- Drop Planters for Nectar           - Slay Werewolf / King Beetle
- Activate Field Boosters            - Run Ant Challenge
- Sync Glitter / Extract Buffs       - Check Sticker Seeker Spawn
```

* **Stack Identical Field Requirements:** Never farm a field for a single quest. Cross-reference Black Bear, Polar Bear, Science Bear, and Bucko/Riley tasks. Farming the Cactus or Rose field when multiple bears require it cuts field time in half.
* **Save Consumables for Science Bear:** Science Bear frequently requires players to craft blender ingredients, burn Glues, or use Moon Charms. Avoid burning these resources early on vanity items or hasty hive rolls until his Translator quests are complete.
* **Macro and Mob Timers:** Player experience shows that setting timers for King Beetle (24 hours), Tunnel Bear (48 hours), and Werewolf (1 hour) ensures you never stall on high-tier combat requirements.
* **Equip the Sticker-Seeker Efficiently:** When running basic field gathering, swap to the Sticker-Seeker occasionally to clear tool-pollen benchmarks while looking out for hidden map decals.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Are BSS Reworked quests mandatory to progress in Bee Swarm Simulator?
While general pollen farming lets you purchase basic hive slots, key storylines like Science Bear (for Translators) and Spirit Bear (for Spirit Petals and Wind Shrine donations) are mandatory for core progression. Completing these milestone quest chains unlocks the mechanics required for late-game gear.

### What is the fastest way to complete Brown Bear scaling quests?
To tackle high-scaling Brown Bear tasks, stack field winds from the Wind Shrine alongside matching Field Boosters and high-tier Planters. Combining these bonuses with a full stack of Focus and Boost tokens allows you to gather hundreds of millions of pollen within a single 15-minute boost window.

### When should I use my Translators in Bee Swarm Simulator?
According to general community consensus and player experience, the recommended order for your three Science Bear Translators is:
1. **Gifted Riley Bee** or **Gifted Bucko Bee** (depending on whether your early hive leans red or blue).
2. The opposing color Quest Bee (Bucko or Riley).
3. **Stick Bug** (as Stick Bug challenges require higher hive levels and active groups to yield worthwhile rewards).

### Do old repeatable quests still exist alongside the updated versions?
No. When repeatable systems are adjusted, the new requirements and prize pools completely overwrite the legacy formats. For example, finishing the 20 Mythic Egg quests permanently switches Black Bear into his modern 1-hour cooldown pool, offering larger pollen requirements alongside scaled endgame honey payouts.
