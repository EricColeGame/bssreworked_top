### Optimizing Unique Instant Conversion
One of the most important mechanics interacting with **BSS reworked bees** is **Unique Instant Conversion (Unique IC)**. Unlike traditional additive conversion, Unique IC applies independently:

```text
\text{Effective Conversion} = 1 - (1 - \text{IC}_1) \times (1 - \text{IC}_2)
```

For instance, having two separate 50% Unique IC sources yields an effective 75% Instant Conversion. With Photon Bee, Demon Bee, Windy Bee clouds, and mutations supplying Unique IC directly, your swarm can convert large pollen loads in the field without filling your backpack instantly.

### Top Mutation Priorities
With the introduction of expanded mutation caps, prioritizing stats on your key **BSS reworked bees** yields noticeable performance gains:
1. **Critical Chance (1%–3%):** Top tier for attack bees, gather bees, and token abilities.
2. **Instant Conversion (8%–20%):** Essential for high-production field farming.
3. **Movespeed (2–6 base):** Maximizes ability collection and token generation efficiency.
4. **Convert Amount (+80 max additive):** High synergy with Crimson and Cobalt gifted pulse procs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which BSS reworked bees gained the biggest overall buffs?
Crimson and Cobalt Bees received some of the most substantial buffs due to level-scaling pulse formulas and instant conversion procs on active field boost stacks. Photon Bee (with base 20% Instant Conversion and x3 Gifted White Pollen) and Tabby Bee (with bonus Critical and Super-Crit stats at max Tabby Love) also gained significant late-game value.

### How did the rework affect bomb and color gifted bonuses?
Previously, bees like Bomber, Rad, Cool, Fire, and Bubble gave additive percentage bonuses (such as +15% or +50%). The rework transitioned these into true multiplicative stats (such as x1.1 or x1.25), creating stronger scaling when combined with endgame amulets, beequips, and field winds.

### What is the advantage of Unique Instant Conversion on reworked bees?
Unique Instant Conversion calculates independently from standard instant conversion pools. This allows players running bees like Demon Bee, Photon Bee, and Windy Bee to convert substantial portions of their gathered pollen on each tick, extending farming time during massive field boost combos.

### Should I keep Puppy Bee after the recent adjustments?
Community reports and player tests show that Puppy Bee is significantly more useful post-rework. The +10% Bee Movespeed and +50% Bee Gather Amount buff from Puppy Love provides noticeable short-term team utility, and the doubled bond payout accelerates hive leveling.
