## Core Mechanics: Understanding Base vs. Multiplicative Pollen

In Bee Swarm Simulator, pollen collection is not a simple flat number. Instead, the game processes your gathering actions through multiple distinct stat categories that combine to form your total yield.

```
Total Harvest = Base Pollen × (1 + Universal Pollen %) × (1 + Color Pollen %) × (1 + Source Pollen %) × Critical Multipliers
```

Universal pollen acts as the baseline scalar for every gather event on the field, whether it originates from your equipped tool, a basic gather action by your bees, or high-tier ability tokens.

| Stat Category | Description | Primary Sources | Application Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Universal Pollen** | Boosts pollen gathered from all sources across every field | Gifted Basic Bee, Bear Morph, Inspire tokens, Amulets | Global Multiplier |
| **Tool Pollen** | Multiplies collection strictly from swinging your collection tool | Stump Badge, Gifted Carpenter Bee, Glue, High-tier gear | Tool Specific |
| **Bee Gather Pollen** | Scales the base gathering output of individual bees on flowers | Gifted Music Bee, Glue, 2x Pollen Gamepass, Mutations | Bee Passive |
| **Bee Ability Pollen** | Amplifies pollen gathered directly by ability tokens (Marks, Flames, etc.) | Gifted Shy Bee, Beequips, Sticker Stack bonuses | Ability Specific |

According to player experience and field testing, neglecting Universal Pollen in favor of purely colored stats results in diminishing returns. Building a solid universal baseline ensures that every specialized buff scales higher.

### Field Capacity Management

When optimizing your setup for **BSS reworked pollen** yields, bag overflow is your biggest bottleneck. Field-specific capacity increases your backpack volume exclusively while harvesting in matching flower environments.

*   **Red Field Capacity:** Increases bag size on red flower fields (boosted by Demon Mask and Riley buffs).
*   **Blue Field Capacity:** Expands capacity heavily on blue fields like Pine Tree (boosted by Diamond Mask and Bucko bonuses).
*   **White Field Capacity:** Scales backpack volume on fields like Coconut and Spider (boosted by Gummy Mask and Polar Bear ornaments).

If your capacity fills in three seconds, your bees stop collecting efficiently, causing significant pollen waste.

## The Critical Pipeline: Critical Hits and Super-Crits

The collection formula features two layers of critical bonuses: standard Critical Hits and high-tier Super-Crits. When properly stacked, these statistics multiply your gathering power by hundreds of percent.

### Critical Power Hierarchy

1.  **Critical Chance:** Determines the likelihood (capped theoretically near 88% through normal gear and Focus) that a gather action lands a critical hit.
2.  **Critical Power:** The percentage multiplier applied to a critical strike (scaling up to nearly 700% through Tabby Blessing, Melody, and Looker Bee).
3.  **Super-Crit Chance:** The probability that an already critical strike upgrades into a Super-Crit.
4.  **Super-Crit Power:** An additional scalar applied strictly to Super-Crit events, instantly converting a portion of the collection.

| Critical Stat | Soft/Hard Cap | Primary Equipment & Buff Sources | Strategic Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Critical Chance** | ~88% Max | Focus tokens, Tropical Drinks, Commander Bee, Ant Amulet | Consistency of high-tier gathering |
| **Critical Power** | ~695% Max | Tabby Bee (Gifted + Tabby Love), Melody, Battle Badge | Massive multiplier on base harvest |
| **Super-Crit Chance** | 53% (Challenge context) | Gifted Precise Bee, Precision stacks, Bloxiade, Super Smoothie | Converts high burst gathering into instant honey |
| **Super-Crit Power** | Variable (Beequips/Amulets) | Cog Amulet, Kazoo/Whistle Beequips, Dark Scythe | Endgame multiplier for red and white hive bursts |

## Conversion Mechanics: Turning Pollen into Honey

Collecting billions of pollen is useless if it never converts into spendable honey. The conversion loop consists of instant field conversion and hive-side return conversion.

```
Total Honey = Pollen Collected × Instant Conversion % × (1 + Honey Per Pollen %)
```

### Conversion Breakdown

*   **Instant Conversion:** Converts a percentage of collected pollen into honey directly on the field, preventing your backpack from clogging.
*   **Instant Color Conversion:** Specialized conversion stats (such as Instant Red or Instant White Conversion) stack with universal instant conversion to keep specific colored bags empty.
*   **Convert Amount vs. Convert Rate:** Convert Amount boosts the base unit of honey your bees convert per cycle, while Convert Rate increases the speed and percentage efficiency of that conversion.
*   **Honey Per Pollen (HPP):** A multiplier applied whenever pollen transforms into honey via tools, tokens, micro-converters, or hive stops.

| Conversion Stat | Function | Best Scaling Items / Buffs | Optimal Hive Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Honey Per Pollen** | Multiplies final honey yield per unit of pollen | Moon Amulet, Wealth Clock, Goo Badge, Nectars | Universal across all hive builds |
| **Instant Conversion** | Converts pollen on-field without visiting hive | Gifted Photon Bee, Enzymes, Festive Blessing | Crucial for Red and White burst hives |
| **Hive Convert Rate** | Accelerates conversion when standing at the hive | Pumpkin Badge, Bubble Bloat, Satisfying Nectar | Vital for Blue hives running Macro setups |
| **Goo Conversion** | Converts pollen based on active Goo multipliers | Gummy Mask, Gummy Star, Gummyballer | Essential for Goo quests and White hives |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the biggest mistake players make with BSS reworked pollen stats?
The most common mistake is over-investing in raw tool pollen while neglecting Universal Pollen, Critical Power, and Instant Conversion. Tools only account for a fraction of total gathering in mid-to-late game; ability tokens, flames, and bee gathers provide the majority of your total harvest.

### How does Honey Per Pollen interact with BSS reworked pollen gains?
Honey Per Pollen (HPP) acts as a final conversion multiplier. When you harvest pollen using your **BSS reworked pollen** multipliers, HPP scales the resulting honey output during conversion. Stacking HPP through Moon Amulets and Wealth Clocks significantly increases the value of every gather tick.

### Does Bee Ability Rate directly increase pollen collection?
Yes. Bee Ability Rate shortens the cooldown timer for your bees to produce tokens. More frequent tokens mean higher uptime on crucial buffs like Focus, Melody, Inspire, and field marks, indirectly multiplying your sustained pollen harvest.

### How do field-specific capacity stats help prevent overfilling?
Field-specific capacity stats (Red, Blue, and White Field Capacity) provide massive percentage increases to your backpack volume based on the flower color distribution of your current field. This extra buffer gives your Instant Conversion mechanics enough time to process incoming pollen before your bag hits maximum capacity.
