## Understanding Core Honey Mechanics in BSS Reworked

At its heart, the core gameplay loop revolves around gathering pollen and transforming it into spendable currency. Your character and your bee swarm work simultaneously across colorful flower patches across the mountain. Once your backpack or container reaches maximum capacity, you must return to your hive to deposit the raw pollen, initiating the conversion process.

```
[Gather Pollen in Fields] ➡️ [Fill Container / Backpack] ➡️ [Return to Hive Base] ➡️ [Convert Pollen to Honey]
```

Honey conversion rates depend heavily on multiple layered stats:

1. **Instant Conversion**: A percentage of pollen collected in the field immediately turns into honey without filling your bag.
2. **Hive Conversion Speed**: How rapidly your hive bees convert raw pollen once you step onto your hive platform.
3. **Pollen Multipliers (Color-Specific)**: Red, Blue, and White pollen multipliers increase how much raw material you suck up per swing.
4. **Field Buffs & Winds**: Temporary modifiers that scale pollen yield exponentially in specific patches.
