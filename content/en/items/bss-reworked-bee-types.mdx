## Complete Breakdown of Bee Rarities and Core Mechanics

Every bee in your hive performs three critical tasks: gathering pollen from flower fields, defending against aggressive creatures, and converting gathered pollen into honey at your hive pads. When fine-tuning your roster around **BSS reworked bee types**, you must balance gather speed, conversion potential, and token generation.

Bees sleep when their energy depletes, resting for roughly 15 seconds before returning to work. Notable exceptions include Exhausted Bee and Photon Bee, both of which feature unlimited operational energy.

## Gifted Bees and Transformation Probabilities

Turning your standard swarm into Gifted variants is the most impactful upgrade path in the game. Gifted bees gain heightened individual stats and provide a passive hive bonus that applies account-wide. Remember that duplicate Gifted hive bonuses do not stack, meaning you only need one Gifted unit of each type to claim the multiplier.

## Essential Tips for Managing Your Bee Swarm

To get the highest return on your resources when rolling and leveling your hive, keep these actionable strategies in mind:

1. **Prioritize Tabby Bee First with Tickets:** Tabby Bee gains permanent stat boosts via Tabby Love tokens (up to 1,000 stacks). Gift Tabby Bee early for massive critical honey bonuses.
2. **Never Feed Star Treats to Basic/Rare Bees:** Always save Star Treats for untradeable Event bees like Bear Bee, Photon Bee, and Gummy Bee, as they cannot be gifted through favorite treats.
3. **Roll Basic Bees with Basic Eggs for Gifted Basic:** Rather than spending a Star Treat, buy Basic Eggs in bulk. With a 1 in 287 chance, you will spend far less honey obtaining the vital +20% Pollen hive bonus.
4. **Position Faster Converters at the Bottom:** Place high-converting bees in the bottom rows of your hive structure to shave milliseconds off their flight path to the player pad.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the main advantages of BSS reworked bee types in the current meta?
The balance adjustments give clearer identity roles to each bee tier. Low-tier bees provide essential passive gifted bonuses, while Mythic and Event bees drive active token production, making proper hive planning rewarding for active and passive players alike.

### Which bee has the highest single-target attack stat?
Precise Bee and fully upgraded Digital Bee (with maximum Red Drives) reach the highest raw attack stat at 16 base damage. Among lower rarities, Lion Bee boasts the highest natural base attack at 10.

### Can duplicate gifted hive bonuses stack?
No. Gifted hive bonuses do not stack with duplicate gifted bees of the same type. You only need one Gifted version of each bee type to receive its account-wide multiplier, making a diverse hive configuration ideal for mixed setups.

### What is the fastest way to get a Gifted Mythic Bee?
The most reliable method is feeding the bee a Star Treat or rolling via Star Jelly if your hive already contains all lower-tier gifted variants (using Star Eggs strategically). Alternatively, feeding tens of thousands of the bee's favorite treat offers a 1 in 24,000 chance per treat consumed.
