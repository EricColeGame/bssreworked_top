### Key Gear Attributes

*   **Pollen Collection Rate:** Determines the raw amount of pollen swept per swing from field flowers.
*   **Color Affinity (Red/Blue/White):** Enhances collection efficiency and critical strike rates in matching flower fields.
*   **Storage Capacity:** Limits how much raw pollen you can hold before having to return to your hive or rely on micro-converters.
*   **Bee Ability Rate (BAR):** Increases the spawn frequency of tokens generated by your bees.
*   **Movement Speed & Jump Power:** Vital for dodging hostile field mobs and quickly navigating between upper mountain zones.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most important early-game BSS Reworked equipment to buy?
The **Golden Rake** is widely considered the most impactful early-game purchase. It covers a broad sweeping area across mixed flower patches, providing a substantial jump in pollen generation compared to starter shovels and basic vacuums.

### Should I buy single-color Port-O-Hives or wait for the Porcelain Port-O-Hive?
If you are running a mixed hive, it is generally better to save directly for the **Porcelain Port-O-Hive** due to its superior neutral capacity and universal conversion buffs. However, if your hive is heavily stacked toward Red or Blue bees early on, the respective colored Port-O-Hive can serve as a cost-effective temporary bridge.

### How does BSS Reworked equipment affect token collection?
Higher-tier utility equipment, such as specialized belts and guards, increases both Bee Ability Rate (BAR) and token pickup radius. This allows you to collect ability tokens, field boosts, and micro-converters automatically while maintaining your swing rhythm in dense flower fields.
