## What Are BSS Reworked Boosts and Multipliers?

In *Bee Swarm Simulator Reworked*, boosts are temporary status effects that dramatically increase your pollen gathering rate, honey conversion speed, bee movement, or field-specific yields. Instead of relying purely on your base gear and bee levels, stacking multipliers allows you to harvest flowers at a multiple of your standard output.

Boosts typically fall into four distinct categories:

1. **Field Multipliers (xBoosts):** Direct percentage increases to all pollen gathered within a designated flower patch (e.g., x4 Pine Tree Forest Boost).
2. **Field Winds:** Advanced environmental buffs (e.g., 15x Winds) that enhance instantaneous collection spikes and capacity handling in specific zones.
3. **Market Boosts:** Special commercial buffs tied to particular fields that supercharge overall yield during active market timers.
4. **Global & Event Buffs:** Universal multipliers like the Honeyday Event buff, which enhance all farming activities across the entire map.

```
Total Pollen Rate = Base Tool/Bee Stats × Field Boost Multiplier × Wind Multiplier × Global Buffs
```

When you understand how these mechanics interact, your progression speed skyrockets.
