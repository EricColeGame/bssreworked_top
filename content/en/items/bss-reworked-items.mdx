The mid-tier Porcelain Port-O-Hive received expanded base capacity alongside at-hive conversion perks. ## Consumables, Nectars, and Unique Instant Conversion

Beyond wearable equipment, consumables and field utility items experienced critical balancing sweeps. The introduction of **Unique Instant Conversion (Unique IC)** fundamentally changed how buffs stack: instead of adding additively to existing Instant Conversion, Unique IC applies independently. For example, two separate 50% Unique IC effects yield a combined effective conversion rate of 75% on all collected pollen.

```
Unique IC Formula: Total Conversion = 1 - ( (1 - Buff A) * (1 - Buff B) )
```

### Consumable Item Overhauls

Player experience and community reports consistently highlight how these consumable changes smoothed out solo grinding routines:

| Consumable / Item | Previous Behavior | Reworked Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Honeysuckles** | Flat instant convert | Pollen converted scales with bees: converts pollen equal to 2x bee convert amount multiplied by (1 + Field Boost stacks). Increased drop rates from planters and memory matches. |
| **Whirligigs** | Static movement speed | Now grants a diminishing Hive Convert Rate boost (+100% decaying down to +1% over 20 seconds). |
| **Tropical Drinks**| Focus and field buffs | Now grants an extra +10% Instant Conversion during its duration. |
| **Super Smoothie** | +12% Instant Conversion | Buffed to +20% Instant Conversion, retaining its massive x1.5 Capacity multiplier. |

### Nectar and Planter Tuning

Nectars harvested from planters are vital for sustained boosts. Refreshing Nectar was famously reworked to award 5%–20% Unique Instant Conversion in place of its previous Bee Ability Pollen stat. Furthermore, baseline conversion rates and starting pollen multipliers across all five nectars were doubled to provide immediate impact even at low nectar percentages.

## Best Strategies to Optimize Your Gear Setup

To make the most of all recent item changes, players should adapt their progression strategy:

1. **Prioritize Nectar Maintenance**: Because Refreshing Nectar provides pure Unique Instant Conversion, maintain high planter uptime in fields that yield refreshing nectar to keep your backpack from filling instantly during large boosts.
2. **Sync Boosts with Field Boost Stacks**: Several **BSS reworked items**, such as the Honeysuckle, Petal Shuriken passive, and Tide Popper wave conversion, directly multiply their conversion power based on active field boost stacks (up to 4x). Trigger your consumables when your field boost is at maximum strength.
3. **Plan Crafting Ingredients Ahead**: High-tier equipment crafting now almost universally demands specific Nectar Vials, Hard/Soft Wax, and Tropical Drinks alongside raw honey. Save your nectar vials rather than consuming them randomly.
4. **Leverage New Mutation Rolls**: Take advantage of mutation reworks that introduced Critical Chance, Movespeed, and standalone Instant Conversion to compensate for lost attack stats on reworked backpacks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are BSS reworked items in Bee Swarm Simulator?
**BSS reworked items** refer to weapons, backpacks, masks, boots, consumables, and beequips whose stats, recipes, or passive mechanics have been redesigned by developer Onett to rebalance gameplay, adjust progression scaling, and introduce mechanics like Unique Instant Conversion.

### How does Unique Instant Conversion work on reworked items?
Unlike standard additive Instant Conversion, Unique Instant Conversion applies independently to all incoming pollen. If you have multiple sources (such as the Coconut Belt passive, Clouds, or Refreshing Nectar), each percentage is calculated sequentially, allowing you to convert substantial pollen without hitting additive caps.

### Why were vial requirements added to mask and boot crafting recipes?
Nectar vials were integrated into recipes for endgame gear like the Demon Mask, Diamond Mask, Gummy Mask, Coconut Clogs, and Gummy Boots to connect equipment progression directly with Dapper Bear's planter mechanics and nectar management systems.

### Can old event Beequips like the Festive Wreath be traded now?
Yes. Previous event-exclusive Beequips like the Festive Wreath and Puppy Bee's Reindeer Antlers were converted into standard items. They can now be traded in the Hive Hub, upgraded using Caustic Wax, or removed from permanent storage.
