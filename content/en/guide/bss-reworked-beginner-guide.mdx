## Getting Started: The First 0 to 5 Bees

Your earliest moments in the game set the foundation for your entire journey. When you first spawn in, your top priority is establishing basic income and claiming hive slots.

First, visit the game's code redemption menu. Redeem non-boost codes immediately to obtain starter honey and tickets. Save boost-heavy codes for later stages when your collection rates are significantly higher. Use your initial honey to purchase Basic Eggs from the dispenser and fill the lowest rows of your hive.

```
Starter Routine:
1. Redeem basic codes (save field boosts).
2. Purchase cheapest hive slots & Basic Eggs.
3. Accept Black Bear and Mother Bear starter quests.
4. Gather pollen from double-flower patches.
5. Convert honey near the hive ring.
```

When converting pollen at your hive, position your avatar at the edge of the conversion ring closest to your hive slots. This minimizes the travel distance your bees must fly back and forth, cutting downtime and boosting conversion efficiency.

## Hive Progression and Zone Milestones (5 to 25+ Bees)

Progress in Bee Swarm Simulator is gated by your total bee count. Each threshold unlocks new questlines, shops, and dangerous boss encounters. Any good **BSS Reworked beginner guide** prioritizes steady hive expansion over expensive vanity cosmetics.

### Milestones Overview

| Hive Size | Unlocked Zones & NPCs | Priority Gear to Buy | Key Focus Area |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **5+ Bees** | Panda Bear, Bamboo Field | Starter Bag upgrades, Basic Boots | Panda mob quests, mob dodging |
| **10+ Bees** | Science Bear, Pro Shop | Super-Scooper, Mega-Jug, Parachute | Science Bear quests, Haste/Bomb tokens |
| **15+ Bees** | Polar Bear, Mountain Top access | Propeller Hat, Port-O-Hive | King Beetle, Werewolf, boost codes |
| **20+ Bees** | Ant Challenge, Star Hall | Ant Amulets, Golden Rake (optional) | Ant passes, Bronze Star prep |
| **25+ Bees** | Ace Shop zone, High-tier Gates | Red/Blue Guards, High-tier Belts | Mid-game crafting, Tunnel Bear |

### 5 to 10 Bees: Quest Prioritization and Mob Tactics
Once you reach 5 bees, Panda Bear offers quests focused on mob defeat. Prioritize Panda Bear quests over Black Bear, but do not kill mobs randomly. Community reports highlight that the Spider has a 30-minute respawn timer; killing it before a quest requires it will stall your progress.

When dealing with aggressive field mobs like Ladybugs, Rhino Beetles, or Spiders, exploit their AI:
- Mobs do not chase you while you are jumping in mid-air.
- Mobs cannot exit their designated field boundaries. Lure them to the perimeter and dance across the border while your bees attack safely.

### 10 to 15 Bees: Tool Efficiency and Mobility
At 10 bees, visit the Pro Shop. Community data indicates that skipping intermediate collectors like the Vacuum saves millions in honey. Instead, purchase the **Super-Scooper** and **Mega-Jug**, followed immediately by the **Parachute**. 

The Parachute gives gliding mobility equivalent to 5–6 stacks of Haste, letting you claim hidden Royal Jellies scattered across high ledges.

```
Early Gear Purchase Flow:
Basic Rake -> Super-Scooper -> Mega-Jug -> Parachute -> Propeller Hat -> Port-O-Hive
```

## Bee Swarm Composition and Ability Token Tier List

A common trap for new beekeepers is hoarding basic bees or rolling aimlessly with Royal Jellies. To build an efficient hive, you must understand token synergies, conversion speed, and ability generation.

### Beginner Hive Tier List

| Tier Level | Bee Classifications | Primary Roles & Abilities | Ideal Hive Quantity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 0** | Event Bees (Tabby, Photon, Bear), Unique Gifteds | Game-changing passives, permanent hive buffs | 1 of each unlocked |
| **Tier 1** | Music, Baby, Diamond, Rage, Commander, Looker | Critical boost (Melody, Baby Love, Focus, Rage) | 2–3 copies each |
| **Tier 2** | Bomber, Demo, Bucko, Riley (Bomb specialists) | Rapid field clearing and explosive pollen gathering | 4–6 total |
| **Tier 3** | Red/Blue Boost generators (Cool, Rad, Frosty) | Color multiplier stacking during field runs | 3–4 total |
| **Tier 4 / 5** | Honey Bee, Brave Bee, Basic Bee (Ungifted) | Filler stats, temporary place-holders | 0 (Reroll ASAP) |

### Balancing Token Distribution
Avoid over-stacking single buffs. Having too many copies of the same bee provides diminishing returns due to token cooldowns and buff caps:
- **Baby Love (Baby Bee):** 2 to 3 bees are plenty for maintaining loot luck and 2x pollen.
- **Melody & Focus (Music, Commander, Looker):** Keep 3 to 4 sources to ensure 100% critical power uptime.
- **Rage (Rage Bee):** 2 to 3 bees give sufficient attack power to clear Ant Challenge waves and bosses quickly.

### Strategic Hive Layout
Bees travel physically from your backpack to their specific hive cell to convert pollen into honey. Place high-conversion bees (like Music Bee) near the bottom of your hive grid. Place lower-conversion bees near the upper slots. This simple layout tweak speeds up overall backpack emptying cycles.

## Resource Management: Honey, Tickets, and Royal Jellies

Managing rare currencies poorly can slow down your progression by several weeks. This **BSS Reworked beginner guide** framework ensures maximum return on your invested resources.

### 1. Spending Honey Smartly
- Prioritize hive slots whenever a new slot costs less than your next meaningful gear upgrade.
- Upgrade basic tools to mid-tier essentials (Super-Scooper, Port-O-Hive) before investing in color-specific guards.
- Avoid buying overpriced single-color gear (like early elite guards) until you produce at least 10–20 million honey per play session.

### 2. Best Practices for Ticket Spending

| Ticket Priority | Item / Bee | Cost | Why You Need It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1st Priority** | Tabby Bee | 500 Tickets | Infinite scaling Tabby Love (+Critical & Conversion). |
| **2nd Priority** | Photon Bee | 500 Tickets | Instant conversion beam; zero bag overfilling. |
| **3rd Priority** | Crimson & Cobalt Bees | 250 Tickets each | Major color boost synergy for mixed hives. |
| **Do Not Buy** | Basic Treats / Ant Passes | Variable | Extreme waste of tickets; farm them naturally instead. |

### 3. Royal Jelly Management
Never waste Royal Jelly rolling on low-level slots if you still have empty Basic Bees. Use Brown Bear daily quests in Clover Field and Ant Challenges to stock up on free jellies. When rolling, aim for a balanced mixed hive rather than chasing rare Mythics early on.

## 5 Crucial Mistakes to Avoid as a Beginner

1. **Picking a Single Hive Color Too Early:** Do not commit to a full Red, Blue, or White hive in early game. Quests require gathering billions of pollen from every field type. Stay mixed until you achieve your Supreme Star Amulet.
2. **Using Boost Codes on Low-Level Gear:** Boost codes that multiply field pollen by 2x or 3x are wasted when your backpack holds under 1 million pollen. Save them for mid-game quest milestones.
3. **Overspending on the Ant Pass Dispenser:** Ant tickets regenerate automatically over time. Spending hard-earned tickets on passes reduces your ability to buy crucial Event Bees.
4. **Ignoring Badge Progression:** Badges provide permanent percentage buffs to pollen collection, movement speed, and capacity. Regularly check your badge progress and work on lagging categories.
5. **Neglecting Treat Leveling:** Leveling your bees increases their hit accuracy against high-level mobs. If your swarm is lower level than King Beetle or Werewolf, their attacks will miss completely.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most important tip in this BSS Reworked beginner guide?
The single most important tip in this **BSS Reworked beginner guide** is maintaining a balanced, mixed hive while prioritizing hive slot expansion over expensive collectors. Staying mixed ensures you complete Black, Science, and Panda Bear quests without bottlenecking on specific field colors.

### Which Event Bee should I purchase first with my tickets?
Always purchase **Tabby Bee** first for 500 tickets. Tabby Bee builds permanent "Tabby Love" stacks every time its ability token is collected, permanently raising its critical power, gather amount, and conversion rate. Buying it early maximizes these lifetime stacks.

### When should I use my Royal Jellies?
Use Royal Jellies early on to transform ungifted Basic Bees into Rare, Epic, or Legendary bees that produce valuable ability tokens like Haste, Focus, and Bombs. Once your hive consists entirely of useful token producers, save excess jellies for Science Bear quests or rolling gifted types.

### How do I kill the Werewolf and King Beetle without dying?
To defeat the Werewolf, trap it by keeping obstacles (such as the field pumpkin or cactus) between yourself and the beast while your bees deal damage. For King Beetle, use your Parachute to float slightly above and to the side of its charge trajectory, staying close enough that your swarm remains engaged.
