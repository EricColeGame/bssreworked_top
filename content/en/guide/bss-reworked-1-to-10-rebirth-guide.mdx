## Understanding the Rebirth Mechanic in BSS 1:1

Unlike traditional progression where gear upgrades provide flat additive bonuses, rebirthing in this overhauled experience applies massive exponential multipliers to your pollen collection, backpack capacity, and conversion rate. Every tier substantially reduces the grind required for subsequent honey totals.

```
Rebirth Multiplier Progression:
Rebirth 1 (x2.25 Pollen)  ──►  Rebirth 5 (x30 Pollen, x2 Loot)  ──►  Rebirth 10 (x750 Pollen, Melittology Tree)
```

Milestone tiers—specifically every fifth rebirth—require you to complete prerequisite questlines before you can prestige. In the early stretch from Rebirth 1 through Rebirth 10, you will encounter two major quest roadblocks: Black Bear’s trial at Rebirth 5 and Mother Bear’s demanding task list at Rebirth 10.

## Early Prestiges: Rebirth 1 Through Rebirth 4 Walkthrough

The opening four rebirths move quickly if you focus purely on honey generation and convert efficiency. Here is how to navigate each step smoothly:

```
Fast-Track Flow:
Basic Hive ──► Rebirth 1 (50K) ──► Rebirth 2 (1M) ──► Diamond Eggs (Rebirth 3) ──► First Mythic Bee (Rebirth 4)
```

### Rebirth 1 (Cost: 50K Honey)
* **Strategy:** Gather from starter fields like Sunflower, Dandelion, and Mushroom. The 50,000 honey threshold takes only a few minutes.
* **Reward Use:** Hatch the **Gold Egg** immediately to expand your workforce with an Epic or better bee.

### Rebirth 2 (Cost: 1M Honey)
* **Strategy:** Take advantage of the x2.25 pollen bonus from Rebirth 1. Focus on higher-tier fields like Clover and Spider Field.
* **Reward Use:** Hold onto your **Diamond Egg** if you plan to build toward diamond gear later, or hatch it early if you need immediate hive strength.

### Rebirth 3 (Cost: 50M Honey)
* **Strategy:** Your capacity jumps to x15, meaning fewer trips back to the hive. Use field boosters and maintain red/blue boost stacks.
* **Reward Use:** Collect 2 additional Diamond Eggs and 50 Tickets. Save the tickets for event bee purchases at the Ticket Tent.

### Rebirth 4 (Cost: 300M Honey)
* **Strategy:** Stack boost tokens in Bamboo and Pineapple fields. Convert rate jumps to x200, making backpack clearing nearly instant.
* **Reward Use:** Hatch your guaranteed **Mythic Egg** to gain a significant hive power spike before taking on the Rebirth 5 questline.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens to my bees and gear when I rebirth?
When you rebirth, you keep your core hive unlocks, equipment, event bees, and materials. The rebirth applies permanent account-wide multiplier buffs to your pollen yield, bag capacity, and honey convert rate.

### How do I complete the 15T from every field task for Rebirth 10 quickly?
Rotate systematically across all lower and upper fields with high-tier planters active. Using loaded dice and matching field boosters ensures you spend minimal time in low-tier patches like Dandelion or Mushroom.

### Is Digital Bee guaranteed from Rebirth 10 in this BSS Reworked 1 to 10 rebirth guide?
Yes. Upon completing Mother Bear's "It's Snack Time!" quest and paying the 40Qa honey cost, Rebirth 10 directly grants 1x Digital Bee along with Melittology Tree access and a permanent x3 loot multiplier.
