## Early Game Roadmaps: From 1 to 15 Bees

When you first spawn into the hive hub, your immediate priority is establishing a reliable foundation of pollen collection and honey conversion. Avoid the temptation to buy expensive cosmetic items or tools that offer marginal upgrades. Instead, direct your earliest earnings into expanding your hive slots.

According to long-standing player experience within the community, buying Basic Eggs from the dispenser is significantly more cost-effective in the early tiers than buying mid-tier tools in the starter shop.

```
Starter Routine:
Talk to Black Bear -> Collect Double Flower Pollen -> Convert at Hive Edge -> Buy Basic Egg
```

| Bee Milestone | Primary Quest Focus | Key Gear / Unlocks | Combat & Field Targets |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **10–15 Bees** | Science Bear & Mother Bear | Super-Scooper, Mega-Jug, Parachute | Pineapple Field, Mantis, Werewolf |

### Optimizing Early Honey Conversion
A simple mechanic that many beginners overlook is positioning during conversion. When your swarm is converting pollen into honey at your hive, stand precisely on the inner edge of the white conversion circle closest to your hive cells. This minimizes the flight distance your bees must travel back and forth, dramatically increasing your conversion per minute.

## Hive Composition and Royal Jelly Strategy

Building an efficient hive does not mean rolling exclusively for Legendary or Mythic bees in the early game. A balanced swarm that chains tokens—such as Haste, Focus, Bombs, and Boosts—will outperform a disorganized swarm of high-rarity bees with overlapping, redundant abilities.

For any player relying on this **BSS Reworked starter guide**, the golden rule is maintaining utility token uptime while placing high conversion bees near the bottom of your hive slots.

### Recommended Bee Ability Ratios (Early to Mid-Game)

| Token Type | Target Bee Count | Key Example Bees | Strategic Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Haste** | 4 – 5 Bees | Hasty Bee, Shocked Bee, Ninja Bee | Maximizes movement speed across large flower fields. |
| **Focus** | 2 – 3 Bees | Looker Bee, Commander Bee, Music Bee | Increases critical gather chance and combat hit potency. |
| **Rage** | 2 – 3 Bees | Rage Bee, Spicy Bee | Essential for passing DPS checks on King Beetle and Ant Challenge. |
| **Bombs (Red/Blue/Colorless)** | 4 – 6 Bees | Bomber Bee, Demo Bee, Bubble Bee | Clears massive clusters of flowers instantly for fast backpack filling. |
| **Baby Love / Melody** | 1 – 2 Bees | Baby Bee, Music Bee | Multiplies pollen intake and increases critical power. |

### Swarm Rarity and Morphing Hierarchy

When applying Royal Jelly to roll for better stats, prioritize morphing your duplicate low-tier bees first.

| Swarm Tier | Bee Classifications | Royal Jelly Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 0 (Never Reroll)** | Event Bees (Tabby, Photon, Vicious), Unique Gifted, Mythics | Lock permanently; feed favorite treats for levels. |
| **Tier 1 (High Priority Keepers)** | Music, Baby, Rage, Commander, Shocked, Looker | Retain optimal ratios (2–3 copies each); reroll excessive duplicates. |
| **Tier 2 (Solid Fillers)** | Bomb-producing bees (Bomber, Demo, Rascal, Bubble) | Keep to maintain steady field-clearing bursts. |
| **Tier 3 (Situational)** | Dedicated single-color Boost bees (Bucko, Riley) | Keep only if your current quests demand heavy single-color pollen. |
| **Tier 4 / 5 (Immediate Reroll)** | Basic Bees, duplicate Brave Bees, excess Honey Bees | Use daily Royal Jellies on these slots immediately. |

## Mid-Game Transition Checklist (20 to 30+ Bees)

Once you reach the 20 to 25 bee threshold, the progression dynamic changes from basic tool upgrades to system synergies, including badge farming, amulet upgrading, and ticket management.

Use this checklist to confirm your account is ready for mid-game content:

- [ ] **Establish Event Bee Priority:** Spend your tickets exclusively on Tabby Bee first, followed by Photon Bee or Crimson/Cobalt Bees. Avoid spending tickets on consumable items like Ant Passes or Basic Eggs.
- [ ] **Secure Your Ant Amulet:** Regularly participate in the Ant Challenge using your free daily passes to improve your amulet tier from Bronze to Silver/Gold.
- [ ] **Unlock Star Hall Access:** Once you discover 5 unique Gifted Bee types, scale the Star Hall to inspect Star Amulet requirements.
- [ ] **Balance Polar Power:** Complete Polar Bear tasks during downtime to stack permanent bee energy buffs across your entire hive.
- [ ] **Optimize Field Boosts:** Redeem promo codes that provide field multipliers only when you have high-capacity backpacks and matching field booster stations ready.

By applying every strategy covered in this **BSS Reworked starter guide**, you will navigate past early roadblocks smoothly, unlock late-game zones faster, and build an optimized hive capable of generating hundreds of millions of honey per session.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most important rule in this BSS Reworked starter guide?
The most vital rule is prioritizing hive slot expansion over minor cosmetic tool upgrades. More bees mean faster gathering, higher combat damage, better token chaining, and quicker conversion speeds.

### Which Event Bee should a beginner buy first with Tickets?
Tabby Bee should almost always be your first ticket purchase. Tabby Bee features a permanent stacking mechanic called "Tabby Love" that scales its gather and critical power throughout your entire playthrough.

### Should I spend tickets on Ant Passes or Royal Jelly in the early game?
No. Tickets are a rare currency that should be reserved almost exclusively for Event Bees and Star Treats. You can obtain sufficient Ant Passes and Royal Jelly through daily quests from Panda Bear and Brown Bear without spending tickets.
