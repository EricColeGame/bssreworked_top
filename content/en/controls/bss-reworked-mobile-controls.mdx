### 1. The Dynamic Harvesting Button

Rather than repeatedly tapping the screen until your finger hurts, the new harvesting trigger supports continuous active harvesting. Holding the harvester button creates a targeted collection radius that matches your scooper's area of effect, allowing for micro-adjustments around flower patches.

### 2. The Contextual Glider Trigger

Previously, jumping near elevated terrain frequently triggered gliders by mistake, throwing off flower collection patterns. The **BSS reworked mobile controls** separate jump inputs from glider deployment, requiring a brief upward swipe on the jump node to engage the parachute or glider.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What makes the BSS reworked mobile controls better than the old system?
The **BSS reworked mobile controls** introduce separated jump/glide triggers, a dynamic movement stick, hold-to-harvest functionality, and an expandable radial consumable wheel. These improvements reduce input latency, prevent misclicks, and eliminate screen clutter during intense farming sessions.

### Can I switch back to the classic mobile layout if I prefer it?
Yes. If you prefer the legacy touch layout, you can navigate to the in-game settings menu, open the *Controls* tab, and switch the input toggle from *Reworked Touch* to *Classic Touch*. However, you will miss out on the radial hotbar and dynamic thumbstick calibration.

### How do I use consumables quickly with the BSS reworked mobile controls?
You can tap and slide outward on the primary utility icon located near the bottom-right action cluster. This opens the radial quick-select wheel, allowing you to trigger items like Stingers, Micro-Converters, and Gumdrops in a single flick motion.

### Does the reworked control scheme support Bluetooth gamepads on mobile?
Yes. When you pair a compatible wireless controller (such as an Xbox or PlayStation gamepad) via Bluetooth, the touch overlay hides automatically and swaps to standard console controller bindings with dedicated button mappings.
