## Graphics, Performance, and Audio Configurations

*BSS Reworked* can produce hundreds of simultaneous particles when multiple bees unleash abilities, pollen blossoms burst, and winds blow across boosted fields. Poor performance can result in missed ability tokens or delayed mob dodging.

Adjusting your graphics within both the game client and your **BSS Reworked settings** ensures stable frame rates even during 15x wind boosts.

| Performance Setting | High-End PC Setup | Budget PC / Mobile Setup | Performance Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Roblox Graphics Quality** | Level 7–10 | Level 2–4 | Very High (affects render distance & FPS) |
| **Bee Ability FX** | Enabled (Full) | Minimal / Low | High (cleans screen clutter in active swarms) |
| **Field Wind VFX** | Full Visibility | Low / Off | Medium (reduces particle overhead) |
| **Sound Effects Volume** | 80% | 80% | Low (vital for hearing token spawns & vicious stings) |
| **Background Music** | 20%–40% | 0%–20% | Low (keeps audio channel clear for cues) |

Community reports suggest that setting the global graphics slider to level 3 eliminates micro-stutters during heavy event phases without sacrificing token visibility.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Where do I find the BSS Reworked settings menu?
You can access the settings menu by clicking the gear icon situated on the left-hand navigation bar of the screen.

### How do I use the code box in BSS Reworked settings?
Open the settings panel using the left sidebar icon. The code redemption input field is located directly at the top of the interface. Paste your active code into the text box and confirm to claim your rewards.

### Does changing graphics settings affect token collection?
Yes. Lowering your particle settings in high-intensity fields prevents frame drops, allowing your character to react faster and gather fleeting tokens before they despawn.

### What are the best camera settings for farming flower fields?
Enabling Shift Lock in your controls combined with zooming out your camera provides the broadest field of view. This setup allows you to track falling tokens, balloon drops, and incoming mob attacks simultaneously.
