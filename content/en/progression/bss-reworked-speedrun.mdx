Before attempting a record, you must understand the rules established by the active player base. In classic Bee Swarm Simulator, external advantages like promo codes, Robux purchases, and booster alts often invalidate competitive integrity. The reworked ruleset levels the playing field so that pure mechanical skill and route optimization dictate the timer.

Community reports and discussions on platforms like the [Bee Swarm Simulator Fandom Community](https://bee-swarm-simulator.fandom.com/) highlight specific bans designed to maintain fair competition across all attempts.

| Rule Category | Allowed Actions | Strictly Banned Actions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monetization** | Private (VIP) Servers for zero-lag routing | Robux avatar scaling/animations, Gamepasses, Robux Honey/Eggs |
| **Account State** | Fresh, unlinked alt accounts, default Bacon/Acorn avatars | Group membership bonuses, Club Dispensers (Honey/Treat dispensers) |
| **Social / External Aid** | Solo play in an empty or private instance | Promo codes, main account jelly bean boosting, friend combat assistance |
| **Timing Criteria** | LiveSplit timer starting on first player input or hive claim | Pausing timers during disconnects, pre-rolling starting eggs |

Enforcing these strict constraints ensures that every **BSS Reworked speedrun** run represents an authentic showcase of game knowledge rather than wallet size.

## Step-by-Step Early Game Quest Routing

Executing a flawless **BSS Reworked speedrun** hinges on overlapping quest requirements. Every second spent running between fields without active, stackable quests wastes precious time.

```
Start Hive -> Claim Basic Egg -> Mother Bear Intro -> Black Bear Quest 1 -> Sunflower Field
```

### 1. The Opening 5 Minutes
As soon as your timer begins, claim the hive closest to the Mountain top ramp to reduce future travel times. Hatch your starter Basic Bee, talk to Mother Bear to unlock basic leveling mechanics, and immediately accept Black Bear's opening task. 

Player experience indicates that feeding your starter bee a single treat immediately unlocks its level-up benefits before you swing your scoop in the Sunflower Field, speeding up your baseline collection rate instantly.

| Step | Action Item | Target Objective | Optimization Tip |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | Claim Central Hive | Minimize travel to shops | Pick Slot 3 or 4 near the ramp |
| **2** | Hatch Basic Bee | Starter collector | Do not spend Royal Jelly immediately |
| **3** | Mother Bear Talk | Unlock treat feeding | Collect the free starter treats |
| **4** | Black Bear Talk | First pollen quest | Stack with Mother Bear's field goals |
| **5** | Tool Upgrade | Purchase Jar & Vacuum | Avoid middle-tier tools; save for Vacuum |

Proper resource allocation separates top-tier runners from casual players. Honey and tickets must be spent strictly on high-return upgrades that compound pollen collection rates.

### Ticket Acquisition Strategies
If your category requires ticket purchases (such as the Cobalt/Crimson Bee sprint), your pathing must incorporate badge tier completions and clock dispensers without violating no-group rules.

*   **Badge Hunting:** Prioritize Pollen badges in Sunflower, Dandelion, and Mushroom fields. Bronze and Silver badge tiers award immediate ticket bundles.
*   **Wealth Clock:** Visit the Wealth Clock every hour on the dot to stack incremental ticket tickets.
*   **Mob Spawns:** Defeat Ladybugs, Rhinos, and Beetles on cooldown; their ticket drop rates provide essential boosts over extended runs.

## Critical Mistakes That Ruin a BSS Reworked Speedrun

Even experienced players can lose minutes due to poor decision-making. Avoiding these common pitfalls is vital when attempting a serious **BSS Reworked speedrun**:

*   **Over-Rolling Basic Bees Early:** Spending early honey on Royal Jellies hoping for a Gifted or Mythic bee drains capital needed for hive slots and backpack expansions.
*   **Ignoring Hive Converter Tokens:** Running back to your hive manually wastes up to 30% of your total run time. Use Field Boosters and Converter tokens to stay on the field longer.
*   **Neglecting Field Badges:** Forgetting to claim badge rewards from the menu denies you instant Honey and Ticket infusions that accelerate tool purchases.
*   **Poor Inventory Management:** Hoarding field items (Gumdrops, Micro-Converters) instead of burning them during major quest spikes slows down your overall split efficiency.

By refining your quest sequence and keeping your inventory lean, you can consistently achieve competitive times in your chosen category.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the fastest category in a BSS Reworked speedrun?
The Cave Monster Honey Egg sprint is generally the fastest tracked category, with optimized runs finishing in under 45 to 60 minutes depending on early combat RNG and bee attack stats.

### Are promo codes allowed in official speedrun attempts?
No. Standard community speedrun rules strictly prohibit the use of promo codes, Robux purchases, and group benefits to preserve competitive fairness and skill-based progression.

### Why is Mother Bear prioritized over Black Bear at the start?
Mother Bear grants immediate treat bundles that allow you to level your starter bee to Level 2 and Level 3 rapidly. Higher-level bees collect pollen and defeat early mobs significantly faster, speeding up subsequent Black Bear tasks.

### Which bee is better to purchase first: Crimson or Cobalt?
In a ticket-based **BSS Reworked speedrun**, your choice between Crimson and Cobalt depends on your dominant hive color rolls. Crimson Bee is generally preferred if your early quests focus on Strawberry and Mushroom fields, while Cobalt Bee excels for Blue Flower and Bamboo field routing.
