## What Is BSS Reworked Rebirth and How Does It Work?

In standard progression fangames, prestige loops provide the foundation for long-term scalability. The rebirth system in *BSS - Reworked* challenges players to trade their immediate resources—such as current honey balance, tool progression, and basic quest states—in exchange for permanent account-level bonuses known as Rebirth Multipliers.

Unlike basic incremental resets, executing a rebirth significantly shortens the time required to clear early-game fields during subsequent runs. It also unlocks specialized vendors, custom mythic bee eggs, and crafting recipes that are completely inaccessible on a baseline save.

## Step-by-Step Preparation Checklist Before Resetting

Never hit the rebirth prompt without preparing your inventory. Executing a **BSS Reworked rebirth** without spending expendable materials results in lost value that could have been converted into permanent character strength.

Follow this checklist before every prestige:

1. **Spend Remaining Honey on Royal Jellies and Treats:** Since raw honey resets to zero, dump your leftover balances into leveling up your persistent bees or stockpiling universal crafting mats.
2. **Claim Completed Quest Rewards:** Ensure all active quest turn-ins are registered. Unclaimed quest turn-ins can disappear when quest logs refresh.
3. **Equip Pollen-Preserving Badges:** Equip titles and amulets that prioritize convert rate and movement speed to accelerate your run back to the 25-Bee and 35-Bee zones.
4. **Log Data Recovery (If Applicable):** For players carrying data from previous versions (such as BSS 1:1 legacy saves), ensure your cloud save profile is synced via the settings menu.

## Hive Strategies for Post-Rebirth Optimization

Selecting the right hive build drastically changes how fast you accumulate the trillions of honey required for subsequent resets. Early rebirths favor mixed hives, while advanced prestige tiers reward specialized single-color builds.

### 1. The Mixed Balanced Hive (Best for Rebirth 1–2)
- **Focus:** Complete general storyline quests and maintain equal red/blue/white pollen gathering.
- **Key Bees:** 3–4 Music Bees (Focus/Melody), 3 Baby Bees (Baby Love), 2 Tabby/Photon Bees.
- **Strength:** Handles every field quest without stalling progression.

### 2. The Blue Bubble/Frost Hive (Best for Rebirth 3–4 Macroing)
- **Focus:** Massive capacity scaling, Bubble bloat, and prolonged conversion loops in Pine Tree Field.
- **Key Bees:** Gifted Buoyant Bees, Tadpole Bees, Diamond Bee.
- **Strength:** Consistent overnight returns and massive raw honey generation per hour.

### 3. The Red Scorch Hive (Best for Rebirth 5+ Burst Grinding)
- **Focus:** Critical hits, flame collection, and instant conversion bursts using high-level Demon and Precise Bees.
- **Key Bees:** Gifted Precise Bees, Spicy Bees, Vector Bees.
- **Strength:** Produces the highest single-session honey spikes in the game when boosted with Super Enzymes.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most efficient time to do your first BSS Reworked rebirth?
Player experience suggests triggering your first reset the moment you reach the 1 Billion honey mark and have unlocked at least 25 hive slots. Holding out longer on your first cycle usually slows down your overall honey-per-hour gain compared to resetting with the new +50% multiplier.

### Do I lose my custom mythic bees after a BSS Reworked rebirth?
No. In the reworked system, custom mythic bees, event bees, and special gifteds are preserved in your account pool or restored upon purchasing your starter slots, ensuring your long-term efforts remain intact.

### How do Rebirth Multipliers stack with badge boosts?
Rebirth multipliers apply as a global account multiplier. This means they multiply your baseline collection rates, which are then further amplified additively by field badges, amulets, and active booster items.

### Can I transfer data from BSS 1:1 into BSS Reworked before resetting?
Yes. According to the developer notes, you can recover compatible player data from *BSS 1:1 made by hk* through the in-game sync utility before starting your initial progression and rebirth loops.
