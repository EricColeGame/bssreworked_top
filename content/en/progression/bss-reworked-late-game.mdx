## Early-Late Game: The 45–47 Bee Transition

Once you cross the 45-bee threshold, raw capacity and conversion become your biggest bottlenecks. At this stage, relying on mixed hive setups starts yielding diminishing returns. You need serious honey reserves to level your bees and unlock the final hive slots.

```
45 Bees + Coconut Gear ➔ Craft Endgame Mask ➔ 40 Gifted Bee Types ➔ Roll First Solo SSA
```

### Essential Milestones for Early-Late Game
1. **Coconut Canister & Clogs**: If you haven't crafted the Coconut Canister, make it top priority. The passive micro-converters and capacity boost are mandatory for late-game fields.
2. **First Endgame Mask**:
   - **Diamond Mask**: Best for macroing and capacity-heavy blue strategies.
   - **Demon Mask**: Massive instant conversion and critical strike power for red collectors.
   - **Gummy Mask**: Balanced white pollen generation and Goo quests.
3. **Gifted Type Hunting**: You must secure 40 unique gifted bee types to unlock the Supreme Star Amulet generator. Use Star Treats strictly on Event bees first, followed by Mythic bees.

| Mask | Best Suited For | Key Passive | Crafting Focus Item |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Diamond Mask** | Blue Hives / Macroing | Diamond Drain (x3 Capacity) | 5 Diamond Eggs, 250 Blue Extracts |
| **Demon Mask** | Red Hives / Attack / Boosts | Ignite & Flame Fuel | 500 Red Extracts, 150 Stingers |
| **Gummy Mask** | Mixed / White / Goo Quests | Gangle & Coin Scatter synergy | 250 Glue, 100 Enzymes |

## Mid-Late Game: Hive Color Specialization and SSA Mechanics

Entering the mid-late tier (47–49 bees) represents the biggest turning point in the **BSS reworked late game** ecosystem. Players should no longer run generalist, mixed hives. You must choose a primary hive color (Blue, Red, or White) to match your Supreme Star Amulet passive.

For official updates and balance patches directly from the developer, check the [Roblox Bee Swarm Simulator Page](https://www.roblox.com/games/1537690962/Bee-Swarm-Simulator) to stay ahead of mechanic adjustments.

### Choosing Your Hive Color

```
                ┌─── Blue Hive (Buoyant/Tadpole) ──> Low Cost, Macro Friendly, Pop Star
Hive Selection ─┼─── Red Hive (Precise/Spicy)     ──> High Cost, Active Play, Scorch Star
                └─── White Hive (Vector/Gummy)     ──> Extreme Cost, High Boosts, Gummy Star
```

- **Blue Hive (Beginner-Friendly Late Game)**: Relies on `Pop Star`. It requires the least honey to become profitable and is forgiving for players who utilize macro farming in Pine Tree Forest.
- **Red Hive (Active Attack & Boosts)**: Powered by `Scorching Star` (or `Scorching + Star Saw`). Requires substantial stingers, red extracts, and precise bee targeting during active boosts.
- **White Hive (Late Endgame Dominance)**: Powered by `Gummy Star + Star Shower`. Incredibly expensive to build due to the need for high-level Vector and Precise bees alongside massive gumdrop consumption.

| Hive Color | Core Mythics Needed | Target Solo SSA Passive | Ideal Double Passive Combo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Blue** | 16–18 Buoyant, 8–10 Tadpole | Pop Star | Pop Star + Guiding Star / Shower |
| **Red** | 10–12 Precise, 8–10 Spicy | Scorching Star | Scorching Star + Star Saw |
| **White** | 14–16 Vector, 6–8 Precise | Gummy Star | Gummy Star + Star Shower |

Navigating this phase in the **BSS reworked late game** meta means committing your honey to Royal Jelly rolling sessions until your mythic composition aligns with your SSA passive.

## Late-Late Game to Endgame: Endgame Tools and Double Passives

Once you hit 50 bees and your hive is fully leveled (Level 16–18+), you enter what community guides classify as Late-Late Game. In this phase, regular gear is replaced by high-tier crafting table weapons.

### Collector Weapons Comparison

Reaching the pinnacle of the **BSS reworked late game** requires obtaining specialized collectors. While the Tide Popper and Dark Scythe require months of crafting mats, the Gummyballer stands at the absolute top of the difficulty tier.

### Perfecting the Double Passive SSA

Rolling a single useful passive on a Supreme Star Amulet costs 10 Billion honey per attempt. Rolling a specific **Double Passive** with high stat percentages (Convert Rate, Critical Chance, Pollen, Instant Conversion) can easily consume hundreds of trillions of honey. 

Player experience indicates that rolling for double passives before securing a Level 16 hive is a common trap; always prioritize hive levels for honey generation before rolling endlessly on the amulet generator.

## Step-by-Step Priority Path for Late-Game Players

To avoid wasting billions of resources, follow this optimized checklist through your late-game journey:

1. **Secure 45 Bee Slots**: Focus pure honey generation on unlocking slots 40 through 45.
2. **Complete Spirit Bear Questline**: Secure all three Spirit Petals (Petal Wand, Petal Belt, Windy Bee donation).
3. **Craft First Endgame Mask**: Choose Diamond for macroing or Demon for attack power.
4. **Discover 40 Gifted Bee Types**: Unlock SSA eligibility.
5. **Roll Solo SSA & Choose Hive Color**: Stop at the first color-matching single passive (e.g., Pop Star for Blue).
6. **Mythic Transformation**: Spend trillions on Royal Jelly to build a single-color mythic hive.
7. **Level Hive to 16+**: Leveling drastically increases gathering efficiency and mob defeat speeds.
8. **Farm Materials for Endgame Collector**: Tide Popper, Dark Scythe, or Gummyballer.
9. **Hunt Double Passive SSA**: Invest surplus honey into top-tier amulet passive combinations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What hive color is best for entering the BSS reworked late game?
Blue hive is universally recommended for early-late game players. It requires only a single passive SSA (`Pop Star`) to generate massive amounts of honey, making it significantly cheaper to operate than Red or White hives, which demand double passives (`Scorching + Star Saw` or `Gummy + Star Shower`) to prevent bag overfilling.

### When should I switch from mixed to a single-color hive?
You should only specialize your hive color once you have unlocked the Supreme Star Amulet (40 gifted bee types discovered) and have at least 5 to 10 trillion honey saved to roll for your required mythic bees and initial solo passive.

### How does the BSS reworked late game differ from older metas?
In modern progression tiers, reaching 50 bees and crafting coconut gear is no longer considered the final endgame. The expansion of endgame tools (Tide Popper, Dark Scythe, Gummyballer), level 18–20 hive scaling, and complex planter crafting have pushed true endgame status far beyond standard 50-bee builds.

### Why is leveling bees prioritized over rolling double passive SSA early on?
Leveling your hive to 16+ significantly boosts bee collection rates, attack damage against bosses, and gathering speed. A high-level hive provides the consistent multi-trillion honey income required to comfortably afford the hundreds of billions—or trillions—of honey needed to roll a viable double passive SSA.
